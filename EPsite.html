  • Agreement that this timeline is the right shape for the site redesign

The 4 phases

What happens in each, who owns it, and what it produces.

Brand workshop

2 monthsSep–Oct 2026Q1–Q2
  • Lock the ASUEP brand standards, including web design standards
  • Settle site strategy before any layout work begins
  • Nothing downstream starts until this is complete

Output: ASUEP brand guide, with the web style guide inside it
Responsible: ASUEP brand with Outreach Hub

Finalize site concept

3 monthsNov 2026–Jan 2027Q2–Q3
  • Site audits, data gathering, information architecture, wireframing and design system creation
  • Copy and content are written alongside concept design
  • Concept is approved before development begins

Output: approved site concept with copy and content
Responsible: Web, Creative, Content with Project Management

Development

2 monthsFeb–Mar 2027Q3
  • Migration strategy, launch plan, SEO, redirects, and technology and security requirements
  • Content is loaded and the site is assembled
  • Runs until the site is review-ready

Output: a built, review-ready site
Responsible: Web, Creative, Project Management

Final reviews + go-live

1 monthApr 2027Q4
  • Stakeholder and leadership reviews
  • Accessibility, proofing, and testing passes
  • Site goes live once approvals are complete; reporting and lessons learned follow

Output: the new site, live end of April 2027
Responsible: Project Management, Web, Creative

What to know

The start gate Nothing begins until the brand workshop opens. Sep 1, 2026 is the working assumption behind every date here.
How much can slip Roughly 2 months. Go-live is end of April 2027 and the fiscal year closes end of June, so May and June are held as buffer.
The handoff to watch Nov 1, 2026. The brand guide has to be usable by then for concept work to begin on schedule.
