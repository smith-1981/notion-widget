Before the clock starts

Site development is dependent on finalizing the ASUEP Brand Standards.

ASUEP brand workshop

Start gateSep 1, 2026Q1
  • The site development start date is set by when this workshop begins
  • Every date that follows moves with this one

Responsible: EP Brand

Direction

Brand and strategy decisions are settled so design has something firm to build against.

EP brand workshop - EP site brand direction and strategy

2 monthsSep–Oct 2026Q1–Q2
  • Lockdown ASUEP brand standards that includes web design standards
  • Settle strategy before any layout work begins
  • Nothing downstream starts until this is complete

Output: EP brand guide, with the web style guide inside it
Responsible: ASUEP/Outreach Hub

Concept phase start

MilestoneNov 1, 2026
  • The handoff point from strategy to creative/web
  • Signals that the brand guid is usable and research, site strategy and design can begin
  • Slipping this date may push out go-live to later in the fiscal year

Responsible: Creative, Web, Project Management

Concept and Development

The site is designed, written, and built.

Concept Phase - Research, Strategy and Design

2 monthsNov–Dec 2026Q2
  • This phase includes site audits, data gathering, information architecture, content, wireframing and design system creation
  • Copy and content are written alongside concept design
  • Concept is approved before web development begins

Output: approved site concept with copy and content
Responsible: Web, Creative, Content with Project Management

Development Phase

2 monthsJan–Feb 2027Q3
  • This phase includes migration strategies, launch plans, SEO, redirects, T&S technical requirements, etc
  • Content is loaded and the site is assembled
  • Runs until the site is review-ready

Responsible: Web, Creative, Project Management

Launch

Final approvals and QA, then the site goes live before the fiscal year closes.

Final reviews and go-live

1 monthMar 2027Q3
  • Final Stakeholder and leadership reviews
  • Accessibility, proofing, and testing passes
  • Launch once approvals are complete

Responsible: Project Management, Web, Creative

Go-live

MilestoneEnd of Mar 2027Q3
  • Site is live and project is complete
  • Reporting and lessons learned follow launch

Responsible: Project Management
